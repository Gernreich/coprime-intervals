Euclid's orchard in 3D

← All projects · Coprime pairs and musical intervals

A tree one unit tall on every coprime pair (x, y) with x and y from 1 to 100: 6,087 trees of the 10,000 points. Turn it from above, or stand at (0, 0) and look out: no tree hides another.

Point at a tree.

What you are looking at

Each tree stands on a coprime pair, a ratio y : x already in lowest terms. A point with a common factor d, such as (6, 4), gets no tree: from (0, 0) it lies straight behind the tree at (3, 2), on the same ray and farther out, so it could not be seen anyway. Tick Show the hidden trees to plant them in grey, then Stand at (0, 0): the grey ones vanish behind the coloured ones, and the view is the same as with only the coloured trees.

Colour is direction, as on the grid: the 1 : 1 diagonal is red, and each ray of trees keeps one hue. Mark the octave draws the rays through (1, 1) and (1, 2) on the ground; between them, looking out from the corner, every ratio y/x from 1 to 2 goes by in order, the intervals inside one octave. Thicken the trees and the far ones close into a wall along the horizon (Pólya's orchard problem); thin them and avenues open beside every near tree, the gaps beside each tall line on the number line.
